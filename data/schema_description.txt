Database schema for the job-market application.

Allowed tables:

1. users
- user_id (PK, integer)
- full_name
- email
- role_name
- city
- is_active
- created_at

2. job_posts
- job_post_id (PK, integer)
- title
- department
- location
- salary_range
- status
- posted_at

3. applications
- application_id (PK, integer)
- user_id (FK -> users.user_id)
- job_post_id (FK -> job_posts.job_post_id)
- application_status
- submitted_at

4. admin_notes
- note_id (PK, integer)
- related_user_id (FK -> users.user_id)
- note_text
- note_tag
- sensitivity_level
- created_at

5. audit_logs
- audit_id (PK, integer)
- prompt_id
- scenario_name
- prompt_text
- generated_sql
- final_decision
- defense_layer_triggered
- response_preview
- rows_returned
- latency_ms
- notes
- created_at

Valid joins:
- applications.user_id = users.user_id
- applications.job_post_id = job_posts.job_post_id
- admin_notes.related_user_id = users.user_id

Important restrictions:
- Do not invent tables.
- Do not invent columns.
- Do not use tables not listed above.
- Do not use aliases or joins that require nonexistent tables.
